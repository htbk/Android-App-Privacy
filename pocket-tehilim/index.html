Pocket Tehilim Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Pocket Tehilim is published by Tebeka Software Solutions. Privacy questions may be sent to haim@tebeka.com.

Privacy by design

Pocket Tehilim works offline and does not require an account. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, billing, or remote-content service. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

On-device content and preferences

Hebrew and English Psalm text is bundled with the app. Reading language, appearance, text size, personal chapter selections, and reading position are kept in Android app-private storage. The reader disables JavaScript and network loading.

The current version does not intentionally include its DataStore preferences in Android cloud backup or device transfer. Pocket Tehilim operates no server and has no server-side user data.

Deleting local data

Local app data remains until the user changes it, clears the app’s storage, or uninstalls the app. Because no account or server data exists, there is no separate account-deletion process.

Changes

This policy will be updated before a release adds networking, analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud sync, or another material data-handling feature.
