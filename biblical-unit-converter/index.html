Biblical Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Biblical Unit Converter is published by Tebeka Software Solutions. Privacy questions may be sent to haim@tebeka.com.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It requests no Internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, media-library, or advertising-ID access. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting service.

On-device content and preferences

Unit catalogs, reference text, and artwork are bundled with the app. Converter selections, language, appearance, and introductory-screen preferences are held in Android app-private storage. Android may include this limited preferences file in a user-controlled operating-system backup or device transfer. Tebeka does not operate that backup service or receive its contents.

Share and Support

Share and Support actions open Android’s system chooser. Nothing is sent unless the user selects another app and confirms the action. The selected destination handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Deleting local data

Users may remove local data through Android’s Clear storage action or by uninstalling the app. There is no developer server or account data to delete.

Changes

This policy will be updated before any material change to the app’s data handling.
