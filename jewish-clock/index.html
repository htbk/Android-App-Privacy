Jewish Clock Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Jewish Clock is published by Tebeka Software Solutions. Privacy questions may be sent to haim@tebeka.com.

Jewish Clock does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, developer backend, or event-sharing feature.

On-device features

Clock calculations, Hebrew and civil dates, holidays, zmanim, alarms, and location calculations occur on the device. Location permission is requested only when the user chooses current location. Selected photos and music use Android’s system pickers; Jewish Clock stores only permission references to the chosen items. Microphone permission is requested only after the user chooses Record a message. Recordings are stored in the app’s private directory and are not uploaded.

Optional stock and news feeds

Stock and news features are optional and off by default. If stock tracking is enabled, Jewish Clock periodically sends the selected ticker symbols directly to Yahoo Finance over HTTPS. If news is enabled, it periodically requests headlines over HTTPS from Fox News, Newsmax, and Israel National News/Arutz 7. Those providers receive ordinary connection information such as the device’s IP address, request time, requested URL, and the JewishClock-Android/1.0 user agent; Yahoo also receives the requested ticker symbols. An IP address may allow a provider to infer approximate location.

Jewish Clock does not send saved coordinates, photos, music, recordings, alarm labels, advertising IDs, or persistent device identifiers with those feed requests. Tebeka does not receive or store the requests or feed responses. Returned quotes and headlines are held in memory for display. The feed providers may retain request records under their own privacy policies.

Local storage and your controls

Settings, saved coordinates, ticker symbols, alarm configuration, and selected-media references are stored in app-private preferences and may be included in Android’s user-controlled backup or device transfer. App-created recordings are deliberately excluded from backup.

Disabling the stock or news option stops the corresponding requests. Local data remains until changed, deleted, cleared through Android Settings, or removed by uninstalling. Third-party request records are controlled by the applicable feed provider.

Security and changes

Network requests use HTTPS. This policy will be updated before any material change to Jewish Clock’s data handling.
